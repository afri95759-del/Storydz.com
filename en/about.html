📜 About Us

Documenting the Greatest Stories in History with Credibility and Passion

Stories Online (storydz.com) is an independent platform specialized in documenting and publishing the most fascinating and important historical stories from around the world. We cover wars that changed the world map, assassinations that shook nations, revolutions that toppled thrones, and mysteries that have puzzled scientists throughout the ages.

Our Mission: To deliver accurate, engaging, and reliable historical content that conveys the truth as it is, while maintaining objectivity and neutrality. We believe that history is the mirror of the present, and that understanding the past is the key to building the future.

🎯 What We Offer

We cover more than 15 main sections on our website, including: Major war stories, details of famous assassinations, revolutions and coups that changed the fate of peoples, historical mysteries that remain unsolved, Islamic and Ottoman history, modern conflicts in the Middle East, stories of colonialism and resistance, the Crusades, world figures who made history, and forgotten African conflicts.

📚 Our Documentation Methodology

We rely on reliable and diverse sources for writing our stories: accredited historical books, original manuscripts, official documents, and expert and academic testimonies. Each story is reviewed by a team of specialized researchers before publication to ensure accuracy and objectivity.

💡 Our Vision for the Future

We aspire to become the largest digital archive of historical stories in the Arabic language and to reach millions of readers around the world. We plan to launch a podcast dedicated to our stories, a YouTube channel offering visual content, and a mobile application.
